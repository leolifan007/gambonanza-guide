Strategy Guide-Rated Builds & Proven Tactics

THE GAMBONANZA MINDSET
This is NOT chess. It's a deckbuilding roguelike that pretends to be chess. If you approach it like a chess game, you lose. If you approach it like Slay the Spire with pieces, you win. This guide is built on that principle.

Opening Builds-Ranked & Explained

Three proven openers. Pick one per run and commit.

Build #1: Knight Aggro S

SStrongest opening. Aggressive knight development into early capture threats.
StepMoveWhy
1Develop both knights to centerKnights on d4/e4 dominate 4x4 and 6x6 boards
2Pawn push to clear diagonalOpens bishop + clears knight’s second L-jump
3Knight+Bishop Combo GambitMaximum reach-threaten 6+ squares simultaneously
4Makibishi’s GambitCaptures drop trap tiles on the defender’s square
5Checkmate with knight+queenFinish before opponent’s board develops
EARLY GAME Strongest turns 1-8. Falls off if game goes past move 15.

Best against: Non-boss boards, Botezarro, Hikarul the Banished
Weak against: Queen-heavy boards (a queen eats aggressive knights), Tal the Cursed

Pro tip:
Pro tip: On 4x4 boards, Knight Aggro can win in 4 moves against the AI. Open d4 knight, e4 knight, then drop a Makibishi’s trap on the defender’s escape square. The AI rarely responds in time on boards smaller than 6x6. This is your speedrun opener.

Build #2: Pawn Wall Defense A

AMost consistent build. Survive early, dominate late. Slower start but higher survival.
StepMoveWhy
1Push center pawns (d4, e4)Control the only squares that matter
2Develop bishops behind pawnsRanged support while pawns block
3Keep a Protective Tile on the kingKing safety and economy protection
4Sacrifice for StrengthTurn pawn wall into mobility buff
5Promote a pawn with Race Flag’sEndgame conversion with a gold payout
MID GAME LATE GAME Weak early, unstoppable late. Needs patience.

Best against: Boss fights (survives longer), Queen Gambit
Weak against: Aggressive AIs that punish slow development

Pro tip:
Pro tip: Pawn Wall + Templar’s and Banner’s protection creates an exploit where the AI wastes ALL its Gambits trying to break through. The AI prioritizes your king, but when it’s protected AND walled, it will burn 5+ Gambits on futile attacks while your bishops destroy everything from behind the wall.

Build #3: Economy Rush B

BHigh-risk build. Prioritize stock over board position. Dominate through purchases.
StepMoveWhy
1Play safe, minimize piece lossesEvery lost piece drains the gold you buy it back with
2Bank cash with Berserker’s, Squirrel’s and Bug Catcher’sCapture and pawn payouts compound your first big win
3Buy gambit tokensA cheap token mid-run finds an economy Gambit without a full shop budget
4Buy S-tier Gambits in bulkOut-gear the opponent
5Win through raw card advantage5 Gambits vs their weaknesses = auto-win
EARLY GAME LATE GAME Risky in mid-game because you're under-equipped. If you survive, you snowball.

Best against: Boss prep runs, long campaigns
Weak against: Early aggressive AIs that don’t let you farm

Pro tip:
Pro tip: The shop always shows the next boss mechanic in the bottom-left corner before you spend a coin. Reading it first is the cheapest edge in the game: it tells you which Gambits are worth buying and which are a waste this run.

Advanced Gambit Combos

Combo #1: The Assassin S

Cards: Makibishi’s + Silver Fork’s + Berserker’s

  1. Move a knight so it threatens 2+ pieces for the Silver Fork’s roll
  2. Trade into the defender with Makibishi’s dropping a trap tile
  3. Silver Fork’s skips the enemy turn when it connects
  4. Berserker’s pays out as your first capture lands

Win condition: Tempo swing with a trap left behind

Combo #2: The Reposition A

Cards: Jump’s + Wrecking Ball’s + Berserker’s

  1. Leap the rook over holes and gaps with Jump’s
  2. Rebuild the rook threat down the open file
  3. If the rook ever dies, Wrecking Ball’s hands you a king
  4. The swing lands in one clean move

Win condition: One move generates 5+ turn value

Combo #3: The Comeback A

Cards: Lemming’s + Throne’s + Queen Ant’s

  1. Open with Lemming’s for a free pawn
  2. Promote with Throne’s so the board gains a king
  3. Farm pawns with Queen Ant’s for gold, then buy back the dead with Battle’s
  4. Buy back the fallen at the Graveyard

Win condition: Full roster rebuilt from gold

Combo #4: The Boss Killer S

Cards: Berserker’s + Throne’s + Dark Knight’s

  1. Read the boss mechanic in the shop preview before you spend anything
  2. Bank gold early with Berserker’s on first-capture turns
  3. Bulk up with Throne’s and Dark Knight’s damage reduction
  4. Drop a trap tile on the square the boss must use to reach you

Win condition: The boss spends its cooldowns while you out-sustain it

A focused 15-card deck of S/A-tier Gambits beats a 35-card deck with mixed tiers every time.

THE GOLDEN RULE OF DECK SIZE

Every Gambit after #22 reduces your chance of drawing your best 5 Gambits. A bloated deck isn’t versatile-it’s diluted. Keep it under 22 cards unless your build specifically requires more.

Reading the Board-Phase by Phase

Turn 1-8: Development

Focus: Get 3+ pieces into active positions

  • Knights first, then bishops, pawns for coverage
  • DON’T bring out queen before turn 5
  • Count enemy Gambits used-they burn them faster than you think
  • If you’re behind after 8 turns, the board was wrong for your build

Turn 9-20: Execution

Focus: Coordinated attacks, Gambit chains

  • Look for 2-in-1 capture lines (Silver Fork’s + Makibishi’s)
  • If ahead: simplify. Trade pieces 1:1.
  • If behind: create chaos. Leap a piece with Jump’s.
  • This is where most games are won or lost

Turn 21+: Endgame

Focus: Checkmate or survive

  • Push pawns to promotion-it’s your best path to victory
  • King activity matters now. Move king toward the action.
  • If winning: ladder mate with rooks
  • If losing: stall, look for comeback Gambit

Boss Fights: Special

Focus: Pattern recognition over aggression

  • Scout first move (throwaway piece)
  • Track boss Gambit cooldowns
  • Time your defensive Gambits for the specific turns they matter
  • Survive > Kill fast. Bosses have limited Gambit pools and WILL run out.

Tilt Management

THE 3-LOSS RULE

If you lose 3 runs in a row, STOP. Your decision-making is compromised. Take 10 minutes, review one replay, identify exactly which Gambit you should have bought instead. Come back fresh.

Common tilt mistakes:

  • Buying expensive Gambits out of frustration
  • Rushing into boss fights under-prepared
  • Ignoring shop rerolls when your build is stuck
  • Playing the same opener after it’s clearly not working

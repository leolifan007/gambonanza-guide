Gambonanza Gold Tile Guide - Getting Paid to Land

How Gold Tiles work in Gambonanza. When to land on them, how they interact with Landing-triggered Gambits, and how to turn a forced landing turn into profit.

Gold Tiles, Explained

Gold Tiles pay out when you land a piece on them. There is no spectral piece system and no ghost piece to convert, so treat this page as the real answer: Gold Tiles are a way to get paid for a landing you were going to make anyway.

THE SHORT VERSION

Landing from Stock costs a full turn. A Gold Tile turns that turn into a payout. The skill is timing: land on gold when you needed to land, not just because the tile is shiny.

How Gold Tiles Fit Your Turn

FactDetail
What it doesPays out when a piece lands on it
The costLanding costs a full turn, Gold Tile or not
The upsideA landing you had to make now also earns gold
The trapLanding on gold for no reason hands the enemy a free turn
Pro tip:
Pair gold with a landing Gambit
Landing-triggered Gambits such as Skydiver’s, Spy’s, and Dungeon’s fire when you land a piece. Combine one with a Gold Tile and a single landing turn both repositions a piece and pays out. See the Gambit Chaining Guide.

When to Take the Gold

SituationTake the gold landing?
You needed to land a piece anywayYes, the payout is free
You have a landing-triggered Gambit readyYes, you double the value
The board is cramped and a landing costs tempoOnly if the payout changes your next shop
You are about to fight a bossUsually no, save the turn for the fight

Gold Tiles and the Boss Preview

Bosses change what a gold landing is worth. Hikarul the Banished blocks your Stock, so land the pieces you need before that fight rather than during it. Read the preview in the lower-left of every shop so you know when a landing turn is safe and when it is a liability.

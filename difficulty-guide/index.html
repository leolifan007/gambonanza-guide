Gambonanza Difficulty Guide - What Changes as You Climb

How Gambonanza difficulty works. The six tiers unlock after your first full clear, each adding negative modifiers, and higher tiers hide an extra boss.

Advertisement
You do not choose a difficulty on your first run. The difficulty tiers unlock only after you finish a full clear. So the first run is about learning the board, and the climb starts after that.

Gambonanza gates its difficulty behind progress. Finish a run once, and the higher tiers open up in sequence. Each one stacks negative modifiers on top of the last.

How the Difficulty System Works

The Base Game, Before Any Difficulty

Before modifiers enter the picture, every run has the same shape:

  • A 5x5 board with 3 pieces, growing by a row after each stage you clear
  • 5 stages of 5 games, with a boss every 5th game
  • 8 real bosses with randomized order
  • The Stalemate Counter (3/3 ends a game) and Crumble pressure from turn one

Get comfortable here first. Difficulty is not harder board geometry, it is more pressure on the same systems.

What Higher Tiers Add

Higher tiers do not change the rules, they narrow your margin for error.

Pressure sourceHow it shows up at higher tiers
Negative modifiersEach tier stacks a modifier that works against you
Tighter economyLess room to buy your way out of a bad boss matchup
Harder bossesThe same 8 bosses, but their modifiers bite more
An extra bossThe top tiers hide a 6th boss beyond the standard roster

General Advice for Higher Tiers

  1. Prep for the boss preview, always. At high tiers, buying the wrong thing is the difference between a clear and a wipe.
  2. Keep a buffer. Without spare gold, one bad boss game ends the run.
  3. Match Gambits to your board. A cheap Gambit you trigger every game beats an expensive one that never fires.
  4. Protect your best piece. A piece lost in a boss fight is gone for the rest of the run.
  5. Watch both counters. Crumble and the Stalemate Counter punish slow play more at high tiers.

Books and Badges

If you are chasing completion, the Collection covers roughly 200 entries across six categories (Gambits, Pieces, Tiles, Enemy Modifiers, Bosses, Strains), and the game ships with 53 Steam achievements. Climbing difficulties and filling the Collection go hand in hand: the harder tiers are where the rare entries and challenge milestones live.

Progression Checklist

Before you move to the next tier, check:

  • Can you clear the current tier consistently?
  • Do you read the boss preview before every shop?
  • Do you keep a gold buffer into boss games?
  • Can you name what each of the 8 bosses does to your board?
  • Do you know which Gambits your board actually triggers?

If yes across the board, the next tier is ready.

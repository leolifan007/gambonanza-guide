The price moved from $10 to $8 and the temptation is to call that a buff. It is not. It is a discount, and the only question a discount changes is what else that money could have bought in the same shop.

{{< game-icon name="money" >}}**$2 is exactly one reroll.** Every shop between games offers one random piece, three random Gambits and three random tokens, with a $2 reroll. The Valhalla cut hands you back precisely one extra look.

{{< callout type="verdict" >}}<strong>THE ONE-SCREEN ANSWER</strong>

Buy when you already have the board state that makes the Gambit work and the boss preview does not demand a different answer. Skip when your five Gambit slots are the bottleneck. Wait when the preview or the Max Piece on Board upgrade is the better use of the same money.{{< /callout >}}

{{< diagram src="valhalla-buy-or-skip-154b.svg" alt="Buy skip or wait decision flow for Valhalla's Gambit at $8" caption="Price is no longer the veto. Slot count, board state and the boss preview are." >}}

The [money and shop guide](/economy/) sets the baseline budget; this page is only the buy-or-skip decision for one Gambit at its new price.

## Three Questions, In Order

A cheaper Gambit is still a Gambit, and you can hold five. The first filter is therefore not money, it is slots. If all five are occupied and none of them is expendable, $8 is no cheaper than $10 because there is nowhere to put it. The [Gambits overview](/gambits/) covers the cap and why selling is a real option in this game.

{{< game-icon name="grid" >}}**The second filter is board state.** A Gambit that needs a specific board condition is only worth money if your board produces that condition. This is the same test the [token decision guide](/which-token-piece-gambit-tile/) applies to piece, Gambit and tile tokens: fix the problem you actually have, not the one a tier list was written for.

The third filter is the boss. Every shop before a boss game shows the next boss and its modifier in the lower-left corner, and the [boss preview guide](/reading-boss-preview-shop/) explains how to turn that into a purchase. A discount on a Gambit that does not answer the preview is still money spent on the wrong thing.

{{< callout type="info" >}}<strong>Quick reference, no table needed</strong>

On Free slot, supportive board, preview does not conflict: $8 leaves $2 for a reroll. 
On Five slots full, nothing worth selling: The bottleneck is slots, not cash. 
On Saving for Max Piece on Board: Compare against the [upgrade payoff](/max-piece-on-board-upgrade/). 
On Preview shows a modifier that attacks a different resource: Spend on the counter the preview names. 
On Money at $8 - $9 total: It is now reachable, which is the only new fact. 
{{< /callout >}}

## What the $2 Actually Buys Back

- **One reroll.** The shop reroll is $2, so a buy at $8 and a reroll costs what the Gambit alone used to cost.
- **One less forced sale.** At $10 you sometimes sold a Gambit to afford it. At $8 that trade happens less often, and a sale now pays out correctly after the [1.5.4b Tooth fix](/v154b-fix-checklist/).
- **Nothing else.** The patch changed a price. It did not change the trigger, the effect, the rarity or the boss preview.

{{< callout type="danger" >}}<strong>Do not turn a discount into a rule</strong>

There is no measured win rate in the official note and none is claimed here. If a guide tells you Valhalla's Gambit is now 'worth it in 68% of runs', that number did not come from the 1.5.4b post. Decide from the five lines in the table above and your own board.{{< /callout >}}

The same caution applies to the fix bundled in that post. Winning because an enemy fell used to leave a phantom piece on the board until the next victory; that was a bug and it is fixed. The cursed tile and tile control notes are a better place to think about board leftovers than a purchase decision.

## The Version Check Nobody Does

If the shop still shows $10, your client is behind and every line of this decision is being applied to a different price. The check takes two seconds and belongs before the purchase, not after a bad run.

{{< pro-tip >}}<strong>Say the reason out loud</strong><br>Before you click buy, name the board condition that will make the Gambit fire. If you cannot name it, you are buying the discount, not the Gambit.{{< /pro-tip >}}

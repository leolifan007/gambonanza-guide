# Enigma's Gambit - The Board-Control Gambit Most Players Miss

## Quick Fix

**Enigma's Gambit flips the color of the tile you capture on. BLACK turns WHITE, WHITE turns BLACK. Used well, it rewrites the board one capture at a time.**

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

| Your Setup | What Enigma's Gambit Does | Skill Floor |
|------------|---------------------------|-------------|
| Single capture | Flips one tile's color | Beginner |
| Capture chain on a color lane | Flips a run of tiles in sequence | Intermediate |
| Color-gated engine | Opens or closes synergies on demand | Advanced |

</div>

**Core mechanic:** Whenever you capture on a tile, Enigma's Gambit switches that tile's color. This matters because many pieces, tiles, and enemy mechanics care about square color (bishops, the Cursed boss's tiles, and color-gated synergies).


<img src="/images/guides/enigmas-copy-chain.svg" alt="Enigma's color-flip chain: capturing on a tile flips its color, turning a bad square good and opening color-gated synergies" />

## Why Enigma's Gambit Is Underrated

Most players skip Enigma's because the description sounds narrow - flipping a tile color? Who cares. The players who pick it up find it answers a problem nothing else does: a board strand that is the wrong color for your pieces.

Here is why it actually performs:

1. **Fixes dead squares** - A bishop stuck on the wrong color is dead weight. Flip the lane and it becomes a threat
2. **Breaks enemy setups** - Colors are load-bearing for some enemy mechanics. Flipping a tile can cut a lane mid-fight
3. **Works with any capture build** - It rides on captures you were already going to make. See [Gambit Synergy Chains](/gambit-synergy-chains/) for the best Gambits to pair with Enigma's
4. **Scales with aggression** - The more you capture, the more tiles you rewrite. Late-game power spike

{{< callout type="verdict" >}}<strong>The Truth</strong>

  Enigma's Gambit is at its best when paired with color-sensitive pieces like bishops and with capture-heavy builds. The only reason it has a reputation as B-Tier is because most players use it with the WRONG Gambits. Flaunt it in a build that ignores color and you just wasted a slot.{{< /callout >}}

## The 3 Best Enigma's Builds

### Build 1: The Bishop Rewrite (Easy)

Pair Enigma's with a bishop-heavy board. Every capture flips a tile to a color your bishops can use. The [Pawn Promotion Guide](/pawn-promotion-sustainability-guide/) is a reliable way to build the board you flip.

**Required Gambits:**
- Enigma's Gambit (core)
- A capture payoff like Berserker's or Bug Catcher's
- A protective option like Templar's or Banner's

**How it plays:**
1. Park a bishop on the color you control
2. Capture toward the tiles you want to convert
3. Enigma's flips them to your bishop's color as you go
4. By mid-game your bishop line is unbroken
5. Use the color advantage to trap enemy pieces

**Reliability:** High on 5x5 and 6x6 boards

### Build 2: The Control Loop (Intermediate)

Pair Enigma's with suppression-style pressure. Flip key squares so the opponent loses safe landing spots.

**Required Gambits:**
- Enigma's Gambit (core)
- A trap-tile Gambit such as Spy's or Poacher's
- A capture Gambit that keeps the flips coming

**How it plays:**
1. Drop trap tiles on the squares you want to contest
2. Capture on adjacent tiles to flip their color
3. The opponent's safe squares shrink as the board shifts color
4. Slowly grind them out while building economy

**Reliability:** High on 5x5-6x6 boards. For the full board control philosophy beyond Gambits, the [Board Clutter Priority Guide](/board-clutter-priority/) covers positioning fundamentals.

### Build 3: The Color Rotation (Advanced)

Chain captures across a color boundary so every flip opens the next one.

**Required Gambits:**
- Enigma's Gambit (core)
- 2 capture Gambits with alternating triggers
- A protective or economy Gambit as the off-cycle slot

**How it plays:**
1. Capture on a black square, flipping it white
2. Move to the now-white lane and capture again
3. Keep the flip chain going across the board
4. Each flip re-opens a lane for your bishops

**Reliability:** Very high late in a run (long games favor this build)

## What NOT to Pair With Enigma's Gambit

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

|-------------|--------------|-------------------|
| Color-blind builds | The flips do nothing for you | Bishop or color-gated builds |
| One-time-use Gambits | Enigma's wants repeated captures | Repeatable capture Gambits |
| Pure defensive builds | Passive play never triggers flips | Mixed capture Gambits |
| Board-fill strategies | You need open lanes to capture along | Lane-control Gambits |

</div>

## Pro Tips

- Count your capture rhythm. If you capture every other turn, plan your flips so the lane stays open.
- Never pick Enigma's as your FIRST Gambit. You need board presence and captures before the flips matter. The [Full Gambits List](/gambits/) ranks every Gambit so you know which ones deserve a slot.
- Enigma's works best as your 2nd or 3rd Gambit pick.
- If you run Enigma's, track the board color around your bishops. I've lost games because I flipped a tile the wrong way right before my bishop needed it.

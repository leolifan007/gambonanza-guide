# Gambonanza Board Growth Guide - How the Board Expands Through a Run

## Quick Fix

**You do not choose your board size, and you do not play on 8x8 boards. A run starts on a 5x5 board and grows by a row after every stage you clear.** Playing the opening board like a huge late-game board is the fastest way to lose tempo.

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

| Stage of the run | Board | What changes |
|------------------|-------|--------------|
| Stage 1 | 5x5 | Cramped, every square matters, knights dominate |
| Mid stages | 6x5, 7x5 | More room, longer threats, files start to open |
| Late stages | Larger | Real space, set up bigger traps and longer plans |

</div>

**The golden rule:** the smaller the board, the more every single square and every single move matters. As the board grows, your plans get longer and safer. Adapt to the board you are on, not the one you wish you had.

<img src="/images/guides/board-size-comparison.svg" alt="Board growth in Gambonanza: the board starts at 5x5 and grows by a row after each stage" />

## The 5x5 Opening Board

### Characteristics
- Cramped space means every square is contested
- Knights threaten a large share of the board
- Rooks and bishops need a line to open before they pay off
- Games resolve quickly, so a passive opening starves you of captures

### Best Builds

The [Knight Rush Opener](/knight-rush-opener/) is one of the most consistent ways to convert the cramped opening into a fast finish.

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

| Build direction | Why it works |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Knight + pawn tempo | Knights dominate close-quarters combat |
| Pawn promotion route | A promoted pawn becomes a queen fast on a small board |
| Early economy | Cheap captures convert into money for the next shop |

</div>

### What NOT to Do

- Do not play slow, greedy builds on the opening board. There is no room for them yet.
- Do not spread your pieces thin. On 5x5, one undefended piece is a target.
- Do not ignore the staging phase. You get to see the enemy layout before each game.

### Positional Strategy
- Center squares control the most routes, so fight for them
- Knights on central squares threaten the widest spread
- Keep at least one escape square for your key piece

## Mid-Run Boards (After the First Stages)

### Characteristics
- Each cleared stage adds a row, so space opens up
- Rooks and bishops start to find real lines
- Longer games become possible, but Crumble still punishes stalling

### The Mid-Run Plan

1. **Keep your Gambit direction live.** If a Gambit stopped matching the board, sell it.
2. **Re-check the boss preview** every shop, since the modifier changes what is worth buying.
3. **Add a recovery route** (a Blessed tile, a spare piece, or recovery money) before you need it.

### Gambit Priority as the Board Grows

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

| Priority | Gambit type | Why |
|----------|-------------|-----|
| Core | Tempo or economy (Thunder's, Berserker's) | Reliable value every game |
| Synergy | A second Gambit that multiplies the first | Stacks one direction |
| Flex | Answers for the next boss | Swap this slot as needed |

</div>

## Late Boards

### Characteristics
- Real space means longer threats and bigger traps
- Games can run longer, so economy compounds
- Mistakes are more punishing because you invested more by now

### The Late Plan
- **Protect your carry.** Your best piece is carrying the run, and captured pieces are gone for good.
- **Watch the Stalemate Counter.** If it reaches 3/3, the game ends even if you still have pieces.
- **Keep captures flowing.** A passive late board feeds Crumble pressure.

## Board Growth Cheat Sheet

```
Opening board:  play tight, win tempo, build economy
Growing board:  open lines, extend threats, add recovery
Late board:     protect the carry, keep captures flowing, watch the counters

Key: the board grows by one row per cleared stage, not per game.
```

## Emergency Adaptations

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| Cramped opening, slow build planned | Pivot to a tempo build now, add the slow build later |
| Crumble pressure rising | Land a piece or take a capture to keep tempo up |
| Stalemate Counter climbing | Open a new capture route before it hits 3/3 |
| Boss restricts your Stock | Land the pieces you need before the fight |

</div>

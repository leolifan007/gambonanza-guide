This page described a boss called "King of Spades," which does not exist in Gambonanza.

The real game has 8 bosses, and none of them are chess-piece personas. Each boss changes a rule, such as disabling your Stock, cursing tiles, or making you skip turns.

Go to the [Gambonanza Boss Guide](/bosses/) for the accurate, verified list of all 8 bosses and their modifiers.

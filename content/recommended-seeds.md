## The Opening Pool Comes From Your Collection

> There are no seed codes in Gambonanza. Your starting pieces come from the **Piece Wheel**, and the pool it draws from is your unlocked **Collection**. So the way to improve your openings is to widen your Collection and make better first decisions, not to type in a number.

{{< callout type="verdict" >}}<strong>THE SHORT VERSION</strong>

If you want a specific opening piece, the answer is not a seed, it is unlocking that piece and then using the Piece Wheel deliberately. This page explains how.{{< /callout >}}

The [Basic Reading of the Piece Wheel](/best-seeds/) covers the spin itself. This page goes deeper on the Collection.

## What the Collection Unlocks

The Collection tracks what your future runs can offer you, across six categories:

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

| Category | What unlocking more does |
|----------|--------------------------|
| Gambits | Wider pool in your shop offerings |
| Pieces | More shapes on the Piece Wheel at run start |
| Tiles | More tile modifiers available to buy |
| Enemy Modifiers | More variety in what you face |
| Bosses | Progress toward the full boss roster |
| Strains | Additional run variants |

</div>

It totals around 200 entries. More Collection progress means a richer shop and a more varied set of starting pieces.

## How to Read a Good Opening

Since the reels draw from your Collection, a strong opening is really about fit, not luck.

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

| If you spin | Good first move |
|-------------|-----------------|
| A queen plus pawns | Buy a pawn-tempo Gambit and lean into promotion routes |
| Knights plus pawns | Play for fork threats while the board is small |
| Rooks plus pawns | Wait for a file to open, then control it |
| All pawns | Take an economy or promotion Gambit early to scale fast |

</div>

{{< pro-tip >}}<strong>Restart freely</strong><br>
  Early runs are short. If a spin does not suit the build you want to practice, restarting costs almost nothing. The spin is a starting point, not a commitment.{{< /pro-tip >}}

## Decisions That Beat the Spin

Your starting pieces set the tone. Your first three choices set the run.

1. **First Gambit:** pick one your board can actually trigger. Thunder's Gambit ($9) is the safest default with pawns active.
2. **First shop:** read the boss preview in the lower-left corner before buying.
3. **Spending order:** survival and economy before raw power, because captured pieces are gone for the run.

{{< callout type="tip" >}}<strong>No ranked mode</strong>

  Gambonanza is a single-player roguelike. There is no ranked ladder, no PvP, and no opponent whose rating your choices affect.{{< /callout >}}

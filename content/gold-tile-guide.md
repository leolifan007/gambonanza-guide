## Gold Tiles, Explained

Gold Tiles pay out when you land a piece on them. There is no spectral piece system and no ghost piece to convert, so treat this page as the real answer: **Gold Tiles are a way to get paid for a landing you were going to make anyway.**

{{< callout type="verdict" >}}<strong>THE SHORT VERSION</strong>

Landing from Stock costs a full turn. A Gold Tile turns that turn into a payout. The skill is timing: land on gold when you needed to land, not just because the tile is shiny.{{< /callout >}}

{{< section-divider >}}

## How Gold Tiles Fit Your Turn

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

| Fact | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **What it does** | Pays out when a piece lands on it |
| **The cost** | Landing costs a full turn, Gold Tile or not |
| **The upside** | A landing you had to make now also earns gold |
| **The trap** | Landing on gold for no reason hands the enemy a free turn |

</div>

{{< pro-tip >}}<strong>Pair gold with a landing Gambit</strong><br>
  Landing-triggered Gambits such as Skydiver's, Spy's, and Dungeon's fire when you land a piece. Combine one with a Gold Tile and a single landing turn both repositions a piece and pays out. See the [Gambit Chaining Guide](/combo-chain-guide/).{{< /pro-tip >}}

{{< section-divider >}}

## When to Take the Gold

<div class="synergy-table" style="overflow-x:auto">

| Situation | Take the gold landing? |
|-----------|------------------------|
| You needed to land a piece anyway | Yes, the payout is free |
| You have a landing-triggered Gambit ready | Yes, you double the value |
| The board is cramped and a landing costs tempo | Only if the payout changes your next shop |
| You are about to fight a boss | Usually no, save the turn for the fight |

</div>

{{< section-divider >}}

## Gold Tiles and the Boss Preview

Bosses change what a gold landing is worth. Hikarul the Banished blocks your Stock, so land the pieces you need **before** that fight rather than during it. Read the preview in the lower-left of every shop so you know when a landing turn is safe and when it is a liability.

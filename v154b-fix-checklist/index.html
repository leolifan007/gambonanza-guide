Six Gambit Bugs Fixed in 1.5.4b: A Check-Your-Client Checklist

The 1.5.4b post lists six fixes: Linux stretching, Tooth's Gambit payout, a Valhalla phantom piece, cursed tile expiration visuals, Barter's counter at 0, and Gambit Medal progress after Quick Restart. Here is how to tell each one is gone.

Advertisement
MID GAME

Some bugs cost you a run. Others quietly cost you a Gambit slot. The 1.5.4b fixes are mostly the second kind, which is exactly why players kept misreading them as balance changes for a month.

Six fixes shipped on 30 September 2026. Two were visual or platform issues, four were state bugs that made a Gambit or a progress counter behave as if it were broken, because it was.

THE ONE-SCREEN ANSWER

If a Gambit looked unusable, a sale paid nothing, a medal reset, or a cursed square never seemed to expire, check your client before you change your strategy. Those were all listed as fixes in 1.5.4b, not rules you needed to play around.

The 1.5.1 to 1.5.3 hotfix breakdown handles the earlier reset rules; the six items below are strictly the 1.5.4b fix list.

The Six Lines, Mapped to What You Actually Saw

Quick reference, no table needed

On Tooth’s Gambit wouldn’t give money when selling it: Sell one and watch the counter. On Barter’s Gambit could appear with its counter set to 0 instead of 5: Check the counter when it is offered. On Valhalla’s Gambit could leave a phantom piece after an enemy-fall win: Win by a fall, then start the next game. On Tile Expiration didn’t visually update cursed tiles: Watch an expired cursed square clear. On Gambit Medal progress reset after Quick Restart: Restart once, reopen the medal view. On Linux stretching at certain resolutions: Set a second resolution and compare.

The first four are the ones that changed decisions. A Gambit with a 0 counter is not a weak Gambit, it is a dead slot until the next shop. A sale that pays nothing breaks the sell-for-gold loop entirely. Both got fixed in the same post, so any advice written in September that works around them is now obsolete.

The Barter confusion is worth separating. Patch 1.5.1 made Barter’s trade Gambit reset on the shop after a Graveyard. Patch 1.5.4b fixed the counter appearing at 0 instead of 5. Those are two different problems, and the Barter guide covers the first while this checklist covers the second.

Why a 0 Counter Was Worse Than It Looked

You can hold at most five Gambits. One of them arriving with a 0 counter means you are carrying four working Gambits into a fight while the shop is already behind you. The Graveyard and stalemate reset page explains the surrounding reset rules; the important point here is that this was an initialisation bug, not a hidden cost or a nerf.

  • Do not sell around it anymore. If the counter reads 5 when offered, the fix is live.
  • Do not rewrite your reroll budget. The $2 reroll was never the workaround for a broken counter.
  • Do update old notes. Any checklist that says to skip Barter’s Gambit on sight is describing a bug that no longer exists.

The same applies to the Medal

Gambit Medal progress resetting after Quick Restart was a persistence bug. If you changed your restart habits to protect medal progress, you can drop that workaround. The difficulty guide covers how progress tiers unlock after a first clear, which is a separate system.

A Five-Minute Verification Pass

You do not need to replay a full run to check a patch this small. Work through the shop, one sale, one restart, and one expiring tile.

  • Open a shop and look at any offered Gambit counter. It should read a real value, not 0.
  • Sell a Gambit and watch the money display. It should move.
  • Use Quick Restart once, then reopen the Gambit Medal view. Progress should still be there.
  • Let a cursed tile expire in a boss game. The visual should clear with the expiry.
  • Check Valhalla’s Gambit shows $8. If it shows $10, you are on an older client and none of the above applies.
Pro tip:
Keep fixes out of your tier list
When a patch says FIX, assume the intended behaviour was always the one you are now seeing. Rank Gambits on what they do at their best, not on the month they spent broken.

If a symptom persists after this pass, the FAQ is the right next stop rather than another strategy rewrite, because a fix that did not land is a client or platform issue, not a build problem.

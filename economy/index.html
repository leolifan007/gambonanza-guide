The Economy, Without the Noise

THE SHORT VERSION

Gold comes from winning games, Gold Tiles, and specific Gambits. There are no investment tables, no compound interest, and no hidden catch-up mechanic. A healthy run is one where you can afford one good buy per shop and still have a buffer for a bad boss fight.

Where Your Money Comes From

SourceHow it works
Winning a gameThe main source. You earn gold for capturing every enemy piece
Gold TilesLanding a piece on a Gold Tile pays out
Gold GambitsA few Gambits convert captures or landings into gold
Selling a GambitYou can sell a Gambit you no longer use to recover part of its cost
Pro tip:
The real economy rule
You cannot farm gold forever. Crumble and the Stalemate Counter keep games moving, so every turn spent stalling is a turn you could be capturing and earning. Economy and tempo are the same skill in this game.

How the Shop Works

Every shop offers a fixed layout. Read it before you spend.

SlotWhat it holds
Piece slot1 random piece
Gambit slots3 random Gambits
Token slots3 tokens (piece, gambit, or tile tokens)
RerollA fixed gold cost to refresh the shop
Boss previewThe lower-left corner shows the next boss and its modifier

Because the boss preview is right there, your first decision in every shop should be: can I afford an answer to that modifier and still buy something useful?

Spending Order: What to Buy First

PriorityBuyWhy
1A Gambit your board can trigger every gameReliable value beats a situational power spike
2A piece or tile that fixes your weakest spotOne safe landing square can save a run
3A “Max Piece on Board” upgradeMore pieces on the board is more capture options
4A flex Gambit that answers the next bossSwap it in when the preview calls for it
ABuy survival before power. A piece you lose in a boss fight is gone for the rest of the run, and rebuying it costs more than protecting it would have.

Three Habits That Keep You Solvent

1. Keep a buffer

Never spend down to zero. A small reserve lets you take one good buy after a messy boss win instead of limping through the next stage.

2. Match Gambits to your board

A cheap Gambit you trigger every game is worth more than an expensive one you trigger once a run.

  1. Sell what stopped working. When the board grows or the boss changes, a Gambit that no longer fires is dead gold sitting in a slot.

When You Are Broke

Short on gold before a boss?

Do not panic-buy. Do these in order:

  1. Win the earlier, easier games in the stage to rebuild gold before the boss game.
  2. Sell any Gambit that stopped matching your board. It is a slot and gold back.
  3. Take the Gold Tile landings you skipped when you were racing.
  4. Buy the cheapest thing that answers the boss modifier, not the most expensive thing on the shelf.

There is no hidden catch-up mechanic and no investment table to lean on. Recovery is tempo: win games, sell dead weight, and spend only on what answers the fight in front of you.
